Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 September 2026

Health Dashboard is a personal-use application created to let its owner review health, body-composition, nutrition, recovery, endurance, and training trends. It is not offered as a public medical or health service.

Information the app accesses

The application may access data from services authorized by the account owner, including:

  • Google Health data, such as activity, sleep, recovery-related metrics, and other authorized health data.
  • Nutrition data made available through Google Health, including Cronometer-supplied nutrition data.
  • Withings measurements, including weight and body-composition metrics.
  • Hevy workout and body-measurement data.

How information is used

Data is used only to generate the owner's personal dashboard, calculate trends and summaries, reconcile duplicate or mirrored data sources, and support the dashboard's training and health views. The application does not use health data for advertising or marketing.

Storage and caching

Direct Withings measurements used by the deployed dashboard are stored in a private application database. Other API-derived information may be temporarily cached by the dashboard platform to improve performance. OAuth credentials and refresh tokens are stored as private application secrets and are not intentionally published in the source repository.

Sharing and sale of data

Personal health data is not sold. The application does not intentionally disclose personal health data to advertisers or data brokers. Data is transmitted only to the services and infrastructure needed to operate the personal dashboard and to the connected services explicitly authorized by the account owner.

OAuth permissions

Google OAuth permissions are requested so the application can read the health information needed for the dashboard. Access can be revoked at any time through the Google account's connected-app or security settings. Revoking access prevents the application from obtaining new Google Health data.

Data retention and deletion

Because this is a personal-use application, retention is controlled by the application owner. Stored application data and OAuth credentials can be deleted from the dashboard's private storage and hosting configuration when no longer needed. Data held by Google, Withings, Hevy, Cronometer, or other connected services remains subject to those services' own retention policies.

Medical disclaimer

The dashboard is for personal tracking and informational purposes only. It does not provide medical diagnosis, treatment, or professional medical advice.

Contact

Questions about this application or its use of data can be directed to the support contact shown on the application's Google OAuth consent screen.
